# Editing examples

These fictional examples were written for this plugin. They illustrate editing
choices; they are never evidence to add to a user's draft. Preserve the supplied
facts, caveats, quotations, and intended voice.

## Describe the change

Draft:

> The release delivers an exceptional improvement to the overall reporting experience.

Revision:

> The release lets users save report filters.

Use this revision only when saved filters are established in the source. Otherwise
remove the praise and ask for the actual change.

## Keep the condition attached to the claim

Draft:

> Uploads complete faster. This depends on the network connection and file size.

Revision:

> Uploads may complete faster on stable connections with small files.

Keep any measured limits from the source. A shorter sentence must not turn a
conditional result into a guarantee.

## Put the reader's task first

Draft:

> We have spent the last week preparing the review materials, and we are now ready to receive your comments.

Revision:

> Please comment on the review materials by [deadline].

Use a placeholder when the source does not supply a deadline. Retain the preparation
history if it explains a delay or changes what the reader needs to do.

## Name the missing information

Draft:

> Several people believe the new process is better.

Revision when the evidence is missing:

> [Identify who evaluated the process and what improved.]

Do not invent a survey, named expert, customer quotation, or numerical result.

## Preserve the speaker

Draft:

> I am unsure whether we should enable this for every account yet.

Revision:

> I am not ready to enable this for every account.

Check that the stronger wording fits the speaker's intent. Retain the original
uncertainty when it is meaningful. Do not add personal feelings or experience to
make a passage sound conversational.

## Make the next step explicit

Draft:

> The request could not be completed successfully at this time.

Revision when the cause is known:

> The upload failed because the file exceeds [size limit]. Choose a smaller file.

When the cause is unknown, say the upload failed and provide the supported retry
or support path. Do not guess at the cause.
