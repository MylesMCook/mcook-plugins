# Source and migration notice

The Writer workflow combines this repository's previous clear-writing and
human-writing skills. The MIT notice accompanying the clear-writing material is
preserved at `skills/writer/LICENSE.txt`.

The current cleanup checklist and editing examples in
`skills/writer/references/signs-of-ai-writing.md` and
`skills/writer/references/human-writing-patterns.md` are original plugin text.
They replace earlier cleanup references whose third-party provenance was
incomplete. The examples are fictional and make no authorship-detection claim.

The `elements-of-style/` references contain adapted guidance and modern examples,
not a reproduction of a later Strunk and White edition. William Strunk's early
*The Elements of Style* is identified as public domain in the United States by
[Project Gutenberg, ebook 37134](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37134).
That source status does not establish the status of later editions or every
jurisdiction. The preserved MIT notice covers the imported clear-writing skill;
this notice does not assign new terms to third-party material or Git history.
