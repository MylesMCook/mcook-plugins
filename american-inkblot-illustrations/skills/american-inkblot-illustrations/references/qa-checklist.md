# QA Checklist

Load this after planning, generation, or editing. Check factual fidelity before
visual taste.

First confirm the selected mode. A general illustration request should remain
Inkblot-first; do not grade it against system-portrait density.

## Pass 1: System truth

Apply this pass to system portraits and any image that makes factual claims.

- The plate has one audience question, scope, and abstraction level.
- Every shown component or actor exists in the brief.
- The primary relationship has the correct origin, destination, action, and
  direction.
- State, queues, delay, ownership, supervision, and trust boundaries are shown
  only when material and supported.
- Static configuration is not presented as proven runtime health.
- Inferred or unknown links are omitted or visibly unresolved, never quietly
  promoted to verified.
- Secrets and unnecessary private data are absent from the image and prompt.
- The companion notes contain title, scope, exact relationships, confidence,
  sources, and open questions.

If any factual check fails, fix the brief or prompt before judging style.

## Pass 2: Visual fidelity

- Standalone 16:9 horizontal composition.
- Pure white background.
- Thin, slightly wobbly black hand-drawn line art.
- Inkblot performs the core action and is not decorative.
- Orange carries the primary path; red marks failure/risk; blue marks state,
  feedback, secondary context, or uncertainty.
- Meaning does not rely on color alone.
- The canvas has no title, legend, protocol paragraph, or code.
- The scene is original, weird, useful, clean, and not childish.

**Inkblot-first:** at least 50% empty space; one Inkblot as the main or only
visible subject; 0-2 essential props; 0-3 short labels; no unnecessary
plumbing, background scene, or extra characters. No prop is larger or more
visually prominent than Inkblot.

**System portrait:** at least 35% empty space; one physical mechanism or scene;
roughly 3-6 verified elements; 0-5 short labels depending on need.

## Failure signals

Regenerate or narrowly edit when:

- The image is a Mermaid/C4/UML/network diagram wearing a sketch filter.
- Abstraction levels are mixed because too much was packed into one plate.
- A title such as `System Architecture`, `Workflow`, or `Roadmap` appears.
- Lines or arrows are ambiguous, decorative, or point the wrong way.
- A database, queue, cache, supervisor, backup, or trust boundary was invented.
- Inkblot merely points at boxes, holds a sign, or watches the mechanism.
- Inkblot resembles a mascot, sticker, emoji, child, animal, or caricature.
- Inkblot-first art contains unrequested system machinery, labels, or a busy
  supporting cast.
- An Inkblot-first prop is described as oversized, looms over Inkblot, or
  becomes the focal point.
- A prop becomes the visual hero while Inkblot looks like its assistant.
- The image has dense labels, realistic UI, cloud-logo wallpaper, tiny nodes,
  paper texture, beige, shadows, gradients, or decorative scenery.
- The image is polished, cute, patriotic, generic, or copied from an example.

## Iteration moves

- **Too complex:** remove secondary routes and move them to another plate or
  the truth ledger.
- **Inkblot not primary in Inkblot-first mode:** remove background machinery
  and extra props until Inkblot carries the idea alone or with at most two
  necessary objects.
- **Too diagram-like:** turn nodes into one physical workplace and relationships
  into a pipe, parcel, tray, door, cable, or handoff.
- **Wrong relationship:** correct only the path and preserve the verified
  composition.
- **Uncertain topology:** omit the link or use one blue unresolved edge; state
  the question outside the image.
- **Too cute:** restore the blank serious expression, small scale, and awkward
  useful labor.
- **Text errors:** try one narrow edit; if widespread, regenerate with fewer
  labels and put exact wording in the caption.

## Delivery bar

A strong plate lets a newcomer grasp the one important relationship within a
few seconds, then lets a technical reader confirm the precise claim in the
truth ledger. If the art is friendly but the plumbing is fictional, it fails.
If it is exact but reads like a slide, it also fails.
