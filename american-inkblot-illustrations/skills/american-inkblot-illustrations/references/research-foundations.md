# Research Foundations

This method combines several established ideas without copying their default
visual notation.

## Plugin packaging and image generation

- [Build plugins](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-plugins) defines plugins
  as installable packages that can distribute stable skills and optional apps.
- [Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills) recommends focused
  workflows, progressive disclosure, explicit inputs/outputs, and realistic
  trigger testing.
- [Image generation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/image-generation) recommends
  concrete prompts, short exact in-image text, one targeted revision at a time,
  and clear statements of what must remain fixed.

## System communication

- The [C4 abstraction model](https://c4model.com/abstractions) separates people,
  systems, containers, components, and code.
- [C4 diagram levels](https://c4model.com/diagrams) use different zoom levels
  for different audiences and recommend only the levels that add value.
- The [C4 review checklist](https://c4model.com/diagrams/checklist) requires
  comprehensible scope, element meaning, notation, and directional
  relationships. This skill keeps those exact details in the truth ledger when
  they would overload the artwork.
- [C4 notation guidance](https://c4model.com/diagrams/notation) explicitly
  allows alternatives to traditional boxes and arrows while requiring
  consistent, self-explanatory meaning.

## Messy situations and visible/hidden work

- The Open University's
  [Rich pictures](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/engineering-technology/rich-pictures)
  guidance uses drawing to explore complex, messy situations and recommends
  including both hard facts and subjective information without excessive text.
- The UK Government Office for Science
  [systems thinking toolkit](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/systems-thinking-for-civil-servants/toolkit)
  uses rich pictures to establish scope and viewpoint before formalizing a
  system.
- Nielsen Norman Group's
  [UX deliverables glossary](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-deliverables-glossary/)
  defines service blueprints around customer journeys, frontstage, backstage,
  and support resources. This skill turns those lanes into physical cutaways.

## Derived design decisions

The verified/inferred/unknown taxonomy, external truth ledger, and Inkblot
physical-metaphor grammar are design decisions of this skill. They are not C4,
Soft Systems Methodology, or service-blueprint standards. Their purpose is to
preserve technical honesty while keeping the image sparse and approachable.
