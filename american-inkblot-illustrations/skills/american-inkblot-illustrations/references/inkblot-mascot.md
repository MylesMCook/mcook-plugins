# Inkblot Character

Load this when the character might drift into mascot, sticker, cartoon, or
decoration territory.

## Visual reference

Inspect `../assets/inkblot-reference.png` before final generation or any edit
that may redraw Inkblot, and pass it to the host image tool as a character and
line-style reference when supported. Use its consistent body, eyes, limbs, and
deadpan tone. Do not copy its three-pose arrangement into the final composition.

## Role

Inkblot is an earnest backstage operator doing absurd physical labor that
explains the system. Inkblot is not a logo, guide, observer, or cute sidekick.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black abstract blob, not a human.
- White dot eyes; blank, calm, slightly confused but serious expression.
- Thin legs and only the thin arms needed for the action.
- Slightly uneven hand-drawn outline.
- Body may become part of a switch, funnel, shadow, plug, stamp pad, valve, or
  machine when that makes the mechanism clearer.
- No hair, clothes, detailed mouth, eyelashes, shoes, accessories, ethnicity,
  gender, age, realistic anatomy, or race-coded features.

## Personality

- Serious about a weird job.
- Dry humor, not slapstick.
- Slightly awkward, never stupid.
- Visibly belongs inside the mechanism.
- Deadpan, not cute.
- Usually the main or only visible subject in Inkblot-first mode.

## Actions

Use actions that carry system semantics: receive, route, sort, stamp, patch,
guard, crank, restart, queue, archive, restore, weigh, filter, plug, relay,
untangle, watch, retry, or hand off.

Examples:

- At a proxy, Inkblot throws one rail switch that sends the orange parcel into
  the correct pipe.
- At a queue, Inkblot places arriving tickets on a shelf while a worker removes
  them from the other end.
- At a trust boundary, Inkblot checks a sealed pass before opening a hatch.
- At recovery, Inkblot hears a red alarm bell and turns a restart crank while
  the durable drawer stays closed and safe.

## Fail conditions

Regenerate or rewrite when:

- Removing Inkblot leaves the explanation intact.
- Inkblot merely points at a diagram or holds a sign.
- Inkblot's action contradicts the direction, timing, ownership, or state in
  the truth ledger.
- Inkblot resembles a toy, sticker, emoji, child, animal, human caricature, or
  brand mascot.
- Inkblot becomes shiny, expressive, adorable, heroic, patriotic, or logo-like.
- In a system portrait, Inkblot becomes so large that the verified mechanism
  is no longer clear.
