The linked commit is the import baseline, not a byte-for-byte claim about the
current bundle. Lawful 0.1.1 narrowed Laws of Software activation to substantive
architecture work; 0.2.1 permits implicit activation within that scope. Lawful
0.2.0 removed its optional Codex hook package and updated source notes,
accessibility checks, and research caveats in the three skills. Laws of
Taste includes Codex UI metadata added in this repository.

Each skill retains its own MIT license. Laws of Software and Laws of UX retain
notices explaining the third-party law catalogs that inspired their original
agent guidance. The Taste bibliography in
`skills/laws-of-taste/references/FOUNDATIONS.md` identifies the works behind
its interpretations and states where edition-level evidence is unavailable.

Lawful's manifests, README, changelog, and this attribution file are released
under the plugin-level [MIT License](LICENSE).
